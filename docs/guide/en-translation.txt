@getting-started|Getting started with aezcheck.com
0|Step-by-step instructions: sign in, create a workspace, invite colleagues and install the aezcheck extension to get started.
2|This guide takes you through your first steps on aezcheck.com — from signing in to operating a complete workspace with all your assets and teammates.
3|1. Sign in to your account
4|Visit the aezcheck.com home page → sign in with the account provided by your company. After signing in, you will reach the overview screen, where you can create or switch workspaces by clicking **[Switch workspace]**.
5|Recommended email: a company domain (e.g. @agency.com) — this adds credibility when inviting colleagues.
6|Each account can join multiple workspaces with different roles.
7|2. Create your first workspace
8|A workspace is a separate working environment containing all assets (VIA, Business Manager, ad accounts), payment cards, personnel and activity logs. An agency typically creates 1 workspace per customer to keep permissions and reconciliation clearly separated.
9|When creating a workspace, enter:
10|**Workspace name** — displayed in navigation and reports.
11|**Time zone** — affects daily spending charts.
12|**Default currency** — VND, USD or another currency used to standardize financial reports.
13|Once created, you can quickly switch workspaces in the top-right corner — all data and permissions automatically update for the selected workspace.
14|3. Invite members and assign permissions
15|Go to **Users & Permissions** → **User Management** → **Invite to organization**. Enter an email and select a role:
16|**Admin**: full access to the system. Admins assign customer groups to customer support staff (CSKH) and enable or disable each role's permissions in the Permissions List.
17|**Leader**: a team leader with more permissions than CSKH. Leaders are assigned under **Ad Account Rental Customer Management** → **Groups tab**. A Leader can view and manage all team members' resources. If managing multiple teams, the Leader can access resources across all those teams. Leaders can also access resources belonging to VIA accounts they uploaded themselves.
18|**CSKH**: can only view ad accounts rented by customers they manage. When an ad account is withdrawn from a customer, the CSKH member can no longer see it in the system.
19|Every role change or permission grant/revocation is recorded in the audit log — supporting internal audit requirements and customer contracts.
20|4. Bill download extension
21|The bill download extension lets you download bills from ad accounts accessible to the VIA currently signed in to your browser. A valid key is required to use the extension. Follow these steps:
22|Enter the key provided by your Admin. The extension sends it to the system for verification.
23|Valid key: you can access the main screen.
24|Incorrect or revoked key: access is denied.
25|**Scan VIA** → The extension automatically detects VIA accounts signed in to your browser and displays their **Avatar**, **VIA name** and **UID**.
26|**Get ad accounts** → For each VIA, the extension retrieves ad accounts for which it has permission to download bills, showing the **Ad account ID** and **Ad account name**.
27|Select data. You can:
28|select individual ad accounts;
29|click **Select All** to select everything;
30|search by ad account ID, then select.
31|**Download bills** → Click **Download Bill**. The system processes and downloads the bills.
32|5. Start your daily workflow
33|After these four steps, you are ready. A typical morning routine:
34|Open **Dashboard** to check overnight alerts and yesterday's spending.
35|Check **Assets** — resync VIA/BM/ad accounts if anything has changed.
36|Go to **Risk Monitoring** to address assets with declining health indicators.
37|At the end of the day, open **Finance & Payments** to reconcile spending and bills.
38|Each step above has its own guide — see _“Read next”_ at the bottom of the page.
@dashboard-overview|Ad account overview dashboard
0|Read VIA and ad account metrics, account statuses, trend charts and risk alerts on the overview page.
2|The Dashboard is each workspace's central screen — bringing all key VIA and ad account metrics together in one view. About 30 seconds here each morning is enough to understand the situation.
3|Overview dashboard: asset metrics, ad account statuses and risk alerts.
4|1. Overall KPI cards
5|At the top of the dashboard are 4 core workspace KPI cards. Each displays its current value for a quick view of asset scale and condition:
6|**Connected VIA count** — the total number of VIA accounts linked to the system.
7|**Total ad accounts** — the total number of ad accounts, split into business and personal accounts.
8|**Disconnected accounts** — accounts disconnected from the system because of a checkpoint or an expired token.
9|**Accounts with data sync errors** — accounts connected to the system that encountered an error while data was being retrieved
10|Click any KPI card to open its detailed list. For example, click **Accounts with data sync errors** to see ad accounts that need attention.
11|2. Account statuses
12|This area counts ad accounts by the statuses returned by the advertising platform:
13|**Active** — the account is active and can run ads.
14|**Disabled** — the account has been disabled.
15|**Unsettled** — a payment remains unresolved.
16|**Under Review** — the platform is reviewing the account.
17|**Pending Payment** — awaiting payment.
18|**In Grace Period** — currently within the grace period.
19|**Pending Closure** — awaiting closure.
20|**Closed** — the account has been closed.
21|**Any Active / Any Disabled / Any Closed** — grouped statuses.
22|Each card has an 👁 icon for quickly viewing accounts with that status.
23|3. Status change charts
24|Two charts help you track account changes throughout the month:
25|**Ad account status trends this month** — the number of accounts in each status by day.
26|**Ad account status breakdown** — each status as a percentage of all accounts, with the latest update time.
27|**Legend** — click a status name to hide or show its corresponding chart line.
28|A sudden rise or fall in a line indicates accounts changing status in bulk and should be checked immediately.
29|4. Security risks
30|This area warns of signs that accounts may have been taken over:
31|**Ad accounts with lost access** — ad accounts for which the VIA no longer has administrative access.
32|**Hidden admin count** — the number of unfamiliar administrators added to the account.
33|When everything is safe, both cards show **0** with _No alerts_.
34|5. Time zone and currency distribution
35|Two tables at the bottom summarize account settings:
36|**Ad accounts by time zone** — for example, Europe/Kiev, America/Phoenix, Asia/Bangkok.
37|**Ad accounts by currency** — for example, USD, VND, EUR.
38|Scroll within each table to see the full list.

2|**Finance & Payments** brings all ad account financial figures together: how much has been spent, how much remains, how much is due and which accounts are nearing a deadline. You do not need to open each Facebook account to check.
3|Financial overview: spending statistics, card reports and payment threshold alerts.
4|1. Open the page and select the data scope
5|Go to **Overview** → **Finance & Payments**. The top-right of the welcome panel has 4 controls that adjust figures across the page:
6|**Refresh** (circular arrow icon) — reload the latest data.
7|**Date range** — select the start and end dates to view.
8|**Currency** — choose the display currency, for example VND.
9|**Ad accounts** — view all ad accounts or select specific ones.
10|All figures below change with these selections. If numbers look unusual, check the selected date range and ad accounts.
11|2. Spending statistics
12|The first four cards show the ad accounts' revenue situation:
13|**Ad spend** — total advertising spend during the selected period.
14|**Total invoiced** — the total amount invoiced by Facebook.
15|**Total balance** — the remaining balance, split into **prepaid balance** and **postpaid balance**.
16|**Total payment due** — the total outstanding amount and the number of **ad accounts requiring payment**. You can also filter by **Status** within the card.
17|The **Total payment due** card includes a **Suggested threshold** (for example 80%). Click the ✏ icon beside it to change the threshold.
18|3. Card reports
19|This area summarizes payment cards linked to ad accounts:
20|**Cards connected to ad accounts** — the total number of cards connected for payments.
21|**Total company deposits** — the total amount deposited into cards.
22|**Actual remaining balance** — the total balance left on cards after payments to Facebook.
23|**Facebook refunds** — the total amount refunded by Facebook.
24|**Unidentified transactions** — transactions the system has not matched to an ad account or invoice.
25|If **Unidentified transactions** increase significantly, go to **Payment Invoice Management** → **Bank Invoices** and filter for **Reconciliation mismatches** to investigate.
26|4. Invoice payments & thresholds
27|This area tracks payment results and warns about ad accounts with upcoming payments:
28|**Successfully paid invoices** — the number of paid invoices and their total amount.
29|**Failed invoice payments** — the number of invoices with payment errors and their total amount.
30|**Invoices refunded by Facebook** — the number of refunded invoices and their total amount.
31|**Ad accounts nearing their payment date** — accounts approaching their due date, based on the system's suggested number of days (for example 20 days).
32|**Accounts reaching the prepaid threshold** — prepaid accounts whose balance reaches the suggested level (for example 500,000 VND).
33|**Accounts reaching the postpaid threshold** — postpaid accounts whose spending reaches the suggested percentage (for example 80%).
34|Click the 👁 icon in each card's corner to see its detailed list. On cards with a **System suggestion**, click ✏ to adjust the alert level to your operating needs.
35|Morning routine: check **Failed invoice payments** first, then the prepaid and postpaid threshold cards to deposit funds or pay on time and avoid ad account interruptions.
